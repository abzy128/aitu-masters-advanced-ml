Customer Churn Prediction

Using XGBoost on Telco Customer Churn Dataset

Artem Grents · Abzal Orazbek

Group AAI-2501M

Advanced Machine Learning

Astana IT University, 2026

Introduction

  • Customer churn — loss of clients/subscribers — costs telecom companies billions annually
  • Acquiring new customers costs 5–7x more than retaining existing ones
  • Industry churn rates: 15–25% per year
  • ML enables proactive identification of at-risk customers for targeted retention
  • Goal: Build a binary classifier to predict whether a customer will churn

Dataset

Telco Customer Churn (Kaggle)

  • 7,043 customer records
  • 19 features after preprocessing
  • Binary target: Churn (Yes/No)
  • Train/test split: 80/20 (stratified)

Feature Categories

Demographics gender, senior citizen, partner, dependents

Account tenure, contract, payment method, charges

Services phone, internet, security, tech support, streaming

Preprocessing

  • TotalCharges blanks coerced to 0; customerID dropped; categoricals label-encoded

Technology: XGBoost

What is XGBoost?

  • Extreme Gradient Boosting — optimized gradient boosting implementation
  • Builds an ensemble of decision trees sequentially
  • Each tree corrects errors of the previous ones
  • Regularized objective to prevent overfitting

Why XGBoost?

  • Top performer on tabular data (Lalwani et al., 2022)
  • Handles non-linear relationships and missing data
  • Built-in feature importance
  • AUC of 0.84–0.90 in churn literature

Hyperparameters

200 trees, depth 5, lr 0.1, subsample 0.8

Conclusion

  • XGBoost achieved ROC-AUC of 0.8317 — consistent with literature (0.83–0.85)
  • Contract type is overwhelmingly the strongest churn predictor (5x more important than next feature)
  • Month-to-month customers are significantly more likely to churn
  • Service add-ons (security, tech support) reduce churn risk
  • Business recommendation: prioritize converting short-term to long-term contracts; bundle support services
  • Future work: SHAP explanations, SMOTE for class imbalance, hyperparameter tuning
