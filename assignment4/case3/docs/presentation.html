Introduction

  • Stock price forecasting informs trading strategies, risk management, and portfolio optimization
  • Prices are influenced by macroeconomics, fundamentals, sentiment, and external events
  • Methods evolved from classical (ARIMA) to deep learning (LSTM, Transformer)
  • Facebook Prophet occupies a middle ground: captures trend + seasonality while remaining accessible
  • Goal: Forecast AAPL daily closing prices using Prophet and evaluate with standard regression metrics

Dataset

Yahoo Finance — AAPL

  • Downloaded via yfinance Python library
  • Period: Jan 2020 – Dec 2024
  • 1,258 trading days
  • Target: daily closing price
  • Univariate setup (price only)

Technology: Facebook Prophet

How It Works

  • Decomposable additive model:
    y(t) = trend + seasonality + holidays + error
  • Piecewise linear/logistic growth trend
  • Fourier series for seasonality
  • Automatic changepoint detection
  • Bayesian framework (Stan backend)

Why Prophet?

  • Designed for business time series at scale (Taylor & Letham, 2018)
  • Minimal manual tuning required
  • Best for medium-to-long-term forecasting (Cao & Wang, 2023)
  • MAPE <5% is standard for stock forecasting

Configuration

Weekly + yearly seasonality; changepoint_prior_scale=0.05

Conclusion

  • MAPE of 2.20% — predictions within ~$5 of actual price
  • Negative R² (-0.49) reveals Prophet's known limitation: trend extrapolation doesn't capture short-term variance
  • Prophet excels at trend decomposition and seasonality, not short-term momentum
  • Results consistent with published benchmarks (Sadorsky, 2021; Hansun et al., 2022)
  • Improvement paths:
    • Hybrid Prophet-ARIMA for residual modeling (Nayak et al., 2020: −12–18% RMSE)
    • Add external regressors: volume, S&P 500, VIX
    • Rolling-window retraining for robust evaluation
